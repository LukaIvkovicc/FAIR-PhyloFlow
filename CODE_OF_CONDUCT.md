# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

FAIR PhyloFlow is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically
constructive community. Contributors should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- distinguish evidence, interpretation, and opinion;
- give appropriate credit for ideas, code, data, and review;
- protect confidential or sensitive information;
- report scientific and software errors openly and constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation,
and inappropriate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer using the
contact channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents FAIR PhyloFlow.
